---
title: How content modeling works
description: Understand schemas, entries, singletons, relationships, and channel-neutral content.
---

Velopulent CMS separates content structure from presentation. A collection definition describes fields. Entries provide values. Frontends decide how to render those values.

## Collections describe repeatable ideas

An Authors collection can hold Maya, Arun, and Lena. A Posts collection can hold hundreds of articles. Each collection gives every entry the same editorial form and validation rules.

## Singletons describe one site-wide idea

Site Settings has one value set. Editors update that record instead of creating another. This makes singleton models useful for navigation, contact details, or a home page.

## Relationships preserve shared meaning

A Post relationship points to an Author entry. Updating the author changes the shared record instead of copied author data across posts. Consumers choose whether to fetch or resolve related content.

## Sites form isolation boundaries

Each site owns its schemas, entries, singletons, files, webhooks, deployment triggers, keys, members, and backups. Use separate sites when content, credentials, or teams need separate lifecycles.
